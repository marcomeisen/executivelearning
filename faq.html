Program FAQ

Who is this training designed for?

The program is built for claims-insurtech executives, business leaders, and functional heads who need strategic technology fluency without becoming technical specialists.

How technical is the course content?

Content is executive-friendly and business-first. It focuses on strategic trade-offs, operational impact, and risk implications rather than coding or infrastructure detail.

What learning format works best?

The strongest outcomes come from a blended approach: short self-learning blocks plus moderated workshops and leadership decision simulations.

Can this be customized to our operating model?

Yes. Module emphasis, examples, and workshop scenarios can be adapted to your claims journey, partner ecosystem, and current platform maturity.

Do you include external self-learning links?

Yes. The program intentionally mixes LinkedIn Learning and Udemy resources, so executive teams can reinforce concepts asynchronously.

How long does a full cohort usually run?

Typical cadence: 4 to 6 weeks self-learning prep, 6 to 8 workshop sessions, 2 to 3 weeks strategic application, and one final review session.

What is the main success criterion?

Participants should be able to hold strategic technology conversations with CTO and product leadership and make stronger decisions about platform, AI, and operating model direction.
