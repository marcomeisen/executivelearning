Getting Started & Learning

Who is this learning designed for?

These modules are built for claims-insurtech executives, business leaders, and functional heads who need strategic technology fluency without becoming technical specialists. Perfect for CTOs, COOs, Product Leaders, and CFOs.

How technical is the learning content?

Content is executive-friendly and business-first. It focuses on strategic trade-offs, operational impact, risk implications, and business outcomes rather than coding or infrastructure details.

What's the best way to learn?

The strongest learning outcomes come from a blended approach: short self-learning blocks plus curated external resources and decision simulations. Mix modules at your own pace or follow a suggested pathway.

Can I customize my learning?

Absolutely. You can choose a recommended pathway or create your own by mixing modules freely. Module examples and use cases can also be adapted to your claims operating model and current platform maturity.

Are there external learning resources?

Yes. The modules intentionally mix LinkedIn Learning and Udemy resources, so you can reinforce concepts asynchronously and learn at your own pace.

How long does learning typically take?

Timeline varies based on your pace and pathway. Individual modules take 40-90 minutes. A full pathway typically spans 6-8 weeks, but you can move faster or slower based on your schedule.

What will I be able to do after learning?

You'll be able to hold strategic technology conversations with CTO and product leadership using a shared language focused on operational leverage, scalability, risk, and customer outcomes.
