# Executive Technology Training for Claims-Insurtech Leaders

## Building Strategic Technology Understanding for Claims Platforms, Automation & Ecosystems

---

# 1. Executive Learning Objectives

## After the training, participants should understand:

### Technology & Claims Operating Models

* How a modern end-to-end claims journey works across insurer, customer, adjuster, repair network, TPA, and third-party partners
* Where technology creates measurable operational value — and where human expertise remains essential
* The difference between:

  * digitizing a manual process
  * automating decisions
  * creating scalable claims operations

### Platform & Architecture Thinking

* Why platform architecture directly impacts:

  * growth
  * speed of customer onboarding
  * integration costs
  * scalability
  * valuation multiples
* The business implications of:

  * monolithic systems
  * modular platforms
  * ecosystem architectures
* Why APIs and integrations are strategic capabilities, not IT details

### Data & AI in Claims

* Why claims data is a long-term strategic asset
* How AI actually creates value in claims:

  * fraud detection
  * triage
  * document extraction
  * straight-through processing
* The operational and legal limits of AI in regulated claims environments

### Executive Decision-Making

* How to evaluate Build vs. Buy decisions realistically
* How vendor lock-in happens
* Why scaling from one insurer to multiple enterprise clients changes technology requirements dramatically
* How governance and organizational structure influence innovation speed

---

## Typical Executive Misconceptions the Training Should Correct

### Common Misconceptions

* “Digital claims” automatically means “automated claims”
* Cloud migration alone creates scalability
* AI can replace operational expertise
* Faster development always means better technology decisions
* Integrations are primarily technical problems
* Claims platforms scale like eCommerce platforms
* Buying software reduces strategic complexity
* Cybersecurity is mainly an IT responsibility

---

## Better Questions Participants Should Learn to Ask

### Platform & Architecture

* “What business flexibility does this architecture enable or limit?”
* “How difficult is onboarding a new insurer or partner?”
* “Where could vendor dependency become strategic risk?”

### Automation & AI

* “Which claims decisions are truly automatable?”
* “What data quality assumptions does this AI model depend on?”
* “How do we detect false positives or biased outcomes?”

### Scaling & Operations

* “What breaks operationally if claim volumes triple?”
* “Where are the hidden manual processes?”
* “What operational dependencies still require human intervention?”

### Product & Governance

* “Who owns product decisions versus delivery decisions?”
* “What slows innovation more: technology or governance?”
* “How do we prioritize between customer customization and platform scalability?”

## Core Topics

* Vendor coordination

# What Is Agentic AI? (Executive-Friendly Explanation)

## Traditional Automation

Traditional claims automation follows:

* predefined rules
* static workflows
* deterministic logic

Example:

> “If claim amount < €1,000 → route automatically.”

---

## Agentic AI

Agentic AI systems can:

* interpret goals
* dynamically decide next steps
* coordinate across systems
