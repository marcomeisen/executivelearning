Module 1Operations

Claims Operations & Technology Fundamentals

Create shared understanding of the claims journey and where technology delivers measurable value.

What You'll Learn

  • End-to-end claims lifecycle: FNOL to settlement
  • Triage and routing logic
  • Vendor coordination and workflow handoffs
  • Human workflows versus machine workflows
  • Digitization versus automation

Why It Matters

  • Scanning documents is not automation
  • Portals without workflow intelligence can increase manual effort
  • Bottlenecks and handoffs are the highest-value intervention points

Learning Outcomes

  • Lower loss adjustment expense
  • Faster and more consistent settlement timelines
  • Improved customer experience quality
  • Stronger fraud signal capture early in process
