Module 9Strategy

Agentic AI & Autonomous Claims Operations

Understand how goal-oriented AI systems can reshape claims scalability, economics, and governance.

What You'll Learn

  • What Agentic AI means in claims and how it differs from static automation
  • High-value use cases: FNOL coordination, triage, vendor orchestration, and fraud support
  • Where autonomous workflows add value and where human oversight must remain
  • Operational and strategic benefits, from cycle-time reduction to ecosystem leverage
  • Risk domains including hallucinations, accountability, legal exposure, and reputation impact
  • How claims operating models shift from task execution to supervision and exception governance

Why It Matters

  • Agentic AI can act as an operational participant, not only a support tool
  • Claims economics change when coordination and prioritization become semi-autonomous
  • Executives must define governance boundaries before scaling autonomy

Learning Outcomes

  • Clearer strategic choices on where to deploy autonomous claims orchestration
  • Better leadership alignment on human-in-the-loop versus autonomous decisions
  • Higher confidence in AI governance, escalation paths, and accountability models

Workshop: Designing the Future Autonomous Claims Organization

A leadership team designs an autonomous claims operating model to reduce handling cost, improve FNOL responsiveness, and increase straight-through processing while preserving trust and control.

Simulation focus: decide where AI agents should act, where humans must remain in control, and which governance risks are unacceptable.

Human-in-the-Loop vs Autonomous Decisions

  • Suitable for agentic orchestration: information gathering, triage prioritization, document handling, and coordination workflows
  • Require human oversight: liability disputes, complex injury claims, legal escalation, and reputationally sensitive exceptions

Typical Executive Misconceptions

  • "Agentic AI replaces claims handlers" when it more often augments teams through orchestration
  • "More autonomy always means more efficiency" even though unmanaged autonomy can amplify risk
  • "This is mostly a technology upgrade" despite the operating model and accountability changes required

Better Questions Executives Should Ask

  • Where does autonomous orchestration create differentiated claims value?
  • Which customer interactions should remain human by design?
  • How are AI decisions audited, escalated, and owned across teams?
  • Can our architecture and ecosystem integrations support reliable autonomous workflows?

Strategic takeaway: Agentic AI is a fundamental shift toward semi-autonomous claims operations, not simply another automation layer.
