What Is a Platform?

A platform is a reusable business and technology foundation that lets multiple participants, workflows, services, and integrations operate and scale together.

In claims, this means one core operating backbone can coordinate insurers, TPAs, repair networks, fraud services, payment providers, and customer interactions through shared workflows, APIs, and data models.

Platform vs Software Thinking

  • Software thinking: deliver a point solution for one customer
  • Platform thinking: enable reusable capabilities for many customers
  • Software thinking: project completion mindset
  • Platform thinking: continuous product and ecosystem evolution
  • Software thinking: customer-specific logic everywhere
  • Platform thinking: configuration over custom code where possible

Architecture Choices for Executives

Monolith

  • Pros: Simpler to launch, single-vendor accountability
  • Risks: Slower innovation, difficult scaling, rigid integrations

Modular Platform

  • Pros: Better flexibility, faster innovation, stronger ecosystem fit
  • Risks: Integration governance and coordination overhead

Best-of-Breed Ecosystem

  • Pros: Specialized capabilities and faster access to new tools
  • Risks: Fragmented accountability and inconsistent operations

No model is universally best. The right choice depends on growth ambition, insurer complexity, customization pressure, and operating maturity.

Use Case Spotlight: Partner & Vendor Integration

Repair shops, assessors, and TPAs sync with claims workflows via APIs and orchestration. Integration depth determines ecosystem growth and operating leverage, not just technical implementation quality.

Misconception corrected: integration is a strategic capability that directly affects commercial scalability.

Key Executive Questions

Operational

  • Which integrations are hardest to maintain and why?
  • Where do manual handoffs still limit speed and quality?
  • How exposed are we to specific vendor dependencies?

Investment

  • Does the architecture improve operating leverage over time?
  • Can AI capabilities scale across customers without rework?
  • What breaks first if claim volume or partner volume triples?
