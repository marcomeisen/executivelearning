Module 6Risk

Security, Privacy & Regulatory Risk

Build practical executive ownership of cyber risk, compliance exposure, and ecosystem trust resilience.

What You'll Learn

  • Sensitive claims data categories and handling models
  • GDPR and jurisdiction-specific obligations
  • Cybersecurity governance and incident response ownership
  • Third-party and vendor risk management
  • Access control, auditability, and evidence readiness

Why It Matters

  • Security is business continuity, not only IT hygiene
  • Claims data is among the most sensitive insurer assets
  • Ecosystem growth increases the attack surface

Learning Focus Areas

  • Document exchange and evidence pipelines
  • Medical claims handling and storage
  • Fraud investigation data access
  • Third-party integration trust boundaries
