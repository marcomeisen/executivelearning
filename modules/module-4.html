Module 4Strategy

Build vs. Buy in Claims Platforms

Evaluate technology investments through total ownership cost, strategic control, and scaling risk.

What You'll Learn

  • Buy, build, configure, or partner decision options
  • Vendor lock-in mechanics and escape costs
  • Total cost of ownership over growth phases
  • Enterprise customization and complexity debt
  • Platform economics and valuation implications

Why It Matters

  • Buying software does not remove operational complexity
  • In-house development does not guarantee strategic control
  • Weak architecture choices become expensive at scale

Learning Outcomes

  • MGA and TPA growth strategy
  • Claims SaaS positioning decisions
  • Legacy modernization priorities
  • Investment committee confidence
